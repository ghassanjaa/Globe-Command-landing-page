FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the main menu, tap COMMANDER EDITION, then Restore purchases. You will also find the button on the paywall screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

What does Commander Edition unlock?

All 12 campaign missions and ELITE difficulty, all 177 countries, Diplomacy and the full research tree. The free version includes missions 1–3 and 20 countries.

Is it a subscription?

No. Commander Edition is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

Where is my progress and my saved wars?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your device to iCloud, the backup includes them.

The map is blank or grey

The map imagery comes from Apple Maps and needs an internet connection. Please check your connection and try again.

Performance and battery tips

Use a lower time speed and close other apps you are not using.

Which languages are supported?

English and Arabic. The game follows your phone’s language, and you can also change it in Settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. The game runs in landscape only.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a bug?

Email gassanjaam@gmail.com with your device model, your iOS version and what happened. Thank you!
