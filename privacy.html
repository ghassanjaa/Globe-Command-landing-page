Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Globe Command · Developer: Ghassan Jaam · Effective date: 22 October 2026

In short: we collect nothing.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your game stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Globe Command has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no tracking. The app does not collect, transmit or share personal data, and no data is sold.

What stays on your device

Game progress, saved wars, settings and statistics are stored only on your device.

Maps

The map uses Apple Maps (MapKit). Map tiles are requested from Apple, and Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (Commander Edition) is processed entirely by Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). The developer never receives your payment details, name or contact data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (App Store age rating 13+).

Access and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for you to access or delete on the developer’s side. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
